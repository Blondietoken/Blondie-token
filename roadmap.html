Staking Mechanics — Full Details

*APY depends on total staked amount and reward emissions schedule. Higher TVL → lower individual APY (shared pool).

NFT Multipliers

  • ✦ Blondie Spark Membership (current collection) → 1.5× on top of tier multiplier
  • ✦ Flagship collection (upcoming) → up to 2.0× depending on rarity tier
  • ✦ Multipliers stack: e.g. Diamond lock (1.5×) + Spark NFT (1.5×) = 2.25× effective weight
  • ✦ NFT must be held in the same wallet as the staked $BLONDIE at snapshot time

How Rewards Work

  • ✦ Rewards accrue continuously and can be claimed anytime (no lock on rewards)
  • ✦ Paid in $BLONDIE from the 22% Holder Rewards pool
  • ✦ Emission schedule: higher in first 12 months post-launch, then tapering to sustainable rate
  • ✦ Your share = (your staked amount × multiplier) / total weighted stake
  • ✦ Spark Points boost applies to the Cosmic Miner & task rewards while tokens remain staked

Early Unstake & Penalties

  • ✦ Unstaking before lock ends incurs a penalty on the principal:
  • — 30-day tier: 5% penalty
  • — 90-day tier: 10% penalty
  • — 180-day tier: 15% penalty
  • ✦ 100% of penalties are sent to the burn address (permanent supply reduction)
  • ✦ Already-claimed rewards are never clawed back

Volume Trader Boost (separate from staking)

  • ✦ Weekly on-chain volume snapshot of $BLONDIE trades
  • ✦ Top volume wallets share a portion of the Holder Rewards pool
  • ✦ Stakers who also trade get both staking yield and volume rewards
  • ✦ Designed so active market participants are rewarded, not only passive holders

Team allocation kept deliberately low (5%) with long vesting so incentives stay aligned with holders.
